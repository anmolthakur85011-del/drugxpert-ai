About DrugXpert AI

DrugXpert AI is a pharmaceutical computational research platform designed to support HSP-based drug–polymer compatibility screening.

HSP Principle:
The Hansen distance (Ra) is calculated from dispersion (δD), polar (δP), and hydrogen-bonding (δH) parameters.

Interpretation:
Lower Ra indicates closer HSP similarity and therefore better predicted compatibility.
