About DrugXpert AI
Intelligent Pharmaceutical Research Platform

DrugXpert AI is a computational pharmaceutical research platform designed to support drug formulation and excipient screening.

The HSP Compatibility module uses Hansen Solubility Parameters to estimate the compatibility between pharmaceutical drugs and polymers.

HSP Compatibility Principle: Lower Hansen distance (Ra) indicates closer solubility-parameter matching and potentially better compatibility.
